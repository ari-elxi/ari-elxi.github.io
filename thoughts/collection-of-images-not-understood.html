collection of images, not understood

monolids. crumpled drawings taped to brick walls. the limit of teeth growing teeth growing teeth. xy plane made of linked rats. id, regurgitated by ego. ego, worn like a coat by superego. concept of self, grinded, powdered, sprinkled into neurons within a self. self with fingers pierced at the pulp. thread wriggling out of skin like a half-dried earthworm searching for freedom. flesh hooked onto fibers, thin reddish bits displayed like trophied fish. a child’s art gashed by the word abnormal in bright red boldface. a child’s lips nailed shut. a child’s lips sealed by another nail. a nail undergoing mitosis. a child strung up by itself. strings growing strings growing strings into fractals. split string ends held by neurons. neurons imprisoning rats. rats hiding powdered selves under their fur. powdered selves drawing images of images inside the bright red gash on a child’s art. images of images that, for whatever reason, shall never be understood by anyone. images of images that make no goddamn sense whatsoever. images of images that eventually all come together to scream a exasperated fuck all, if someone even bothered to try to decipher them, if there even is a cipher at all. why don’t you just fucking get it. images of images of the tangled mess of a yearning consciousness wetting its powdered body with brain fluids, kneading itself into messier dough. a dough prying open the ego’s nailed shut mouth. a dough leaping out of the window of the superego’s mansion it calls a body. a leaping suicide in search of a place. somewhere for a stupid fucking dough with its ugly monolids to exist in peace. somewhere.
