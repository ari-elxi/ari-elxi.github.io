Rat Pendulum

In my mind there exists a tangled cloud of undefined concepts, scurrying around like rats in a city too big for them. As I navigate through my life, I found that most people’s thoughts hop around constantly, roaming territories the “outside world” would consider all too weird and meaningless.

Through embracing non-traditional and mixed media in my pieces, including kinetics, silk screen, felting, and painting, I let my instincts take me through an unconfined artistic process, ending with art that conflicts order with disorder. In contrast to the clearly structured world where everything needs some purpose, my work confronts the viewers with a mess made from clean, smooth, yet bright and abstract silhouettes.

Specifically, I highlighted the motif of the rat. As commonly hated critters that do not follow rules we put up, rats survive by hiding in the corners and moving around at night time, much like how we hide those meaningless thoughts that pop up inside our minds. Their prominent representation as well as enlargement in my art, placed against price tags that give them value, brings the insides out. Like a pendulum, my work swings audiences of any background out of this world that they stand in, back into that cloudy world of imaginations in their minds.
